Reclaim Your Heart

My Favorite Book 🧡

Reclaim Your Heart by Yasmin Mogahed is not only a self-help guide. It is a handbook about the journey of the heart throughout the expanse of this life. It is a book about how to hold your heart back from sinking to the depths of that ocean, and what to do when it does. It is a book about recovery, trust, hope, and renewal.

Each heart can recover, and every moment is made to carry us closer to that great return. It is about discovering your own awakening and becoming a freer version of yourself. Many of us live our lives entangled by repeated patterns of heartbreak and disappointment often without knowing why.

Reclaim Your Heart is about freeing the heart from this bondage, navigating life’s traps, and finding renewal. It’s a handbook on how to live without allowing life to possess you, while protecting your most valued possession: the heart.
